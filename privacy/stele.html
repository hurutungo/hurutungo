Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026
Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how hurutungo LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”), the developer of the Stele app (“the app”), handles information when you use the app. The short version: the app does not collect any information, and your recordings never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

If you have any questions, contact us at hello@hurutungo.com.

1. Information We Collect

None. Stele has no accounts and no sign-in, and it makes no network requests. We do not run servers for the app, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. We never receive your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or usage data.

2. Your Recordings

Screen recordings, including anything visible or audible in them, are stored only on your device, inside the app. A recording leaves the app only when you choose to save it to Photos or share it through the iOS share sheet. From that point, the destination you choose handles it under its own policy.

3. Permissions

Stele asks for the following permissions only to provide its features. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings, and the app keeps working without them.

  • Camera — shows your face in the facecam bubble while you record. The camera image becomes part of your recording and is not stored anywhere else.
  • Microphone — records your voice when you turn on the mic in the system recording sheet.
  • Photos (add only) — saves recordings you choose to save. Stele cannot read your photo library.
  • Notifications — lets you know when a recording is ready.

4. Purchases

Stele is a paid app with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple processes the purchase through the App Store under Apple’s privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect, sell, or “share” (as defined under California law) personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. Recordings on your device are under your control: deleting a recording in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. It does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@hurutungo.com.
