Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026
Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how hurutungo LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”), the developer of the Anime Fillers app (“the app”), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. We are the data controller responsible for your information.

If you have any questions, contact us at hello@hurutungo.com.

1. Information We Collect

We deliberately collect as little as possible. We do not ask for or store your name, password, phone number, or precise location. The one exception is an email address, and only if you choose to include one with in-app feedback — see below.

Anonymous account identifier. The first time you use the app, our server creates an anonymous account for you, identified by a randomly generated token and an associated access token (stored in hashed form). This lets the app remember your purchases across sessions without knowing who you are. It is not linked to your real-world identity.

Purchase and subscription records. When you buy a subscription or a one-time purchase, Apple’s App Store processes the payment and sends our server limited details about it — which product you bought, whether a subscription is active, an Apple-generated transaction identifier, and whether the purchase was made in Apple’s production or sandbox environment. We use this to unlock the features you paid for. We never receive or store your payment card, billing address, or other payment details — Apple handles all payment processing.

Technical logs. Like most internet services, our servers automatically record basic technical information when your app communicates with them, such as your IP address and app/device user-agent string, in short-lived operational logs. This is used to operate and secure the service.

In-app feedback. If you submit feedback, a bug report, or a suggestion from within the app, we store the message you write. You may optionally include an email address so we can reply — this is entirely optional, and we do not verify that the address belongs to you or use it for anything other than responding to your report.

Favorites and watch progress. The shows you favorite and your watch progress are stored only on your device. They are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Information and Our Legal Bases

We use the information above to:

  • create and maintain your anonymous account and unlock purchased features (legal basis: performance of a contract — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b));
  • operate, secure, debug, and prevent fraud or abuse of the service (legal basis: our legitimate interests in running a secure service — GDPR Art. 6(1)(f));
  • review and respond to feedback, bug reports, or suggestions you submit, including replying to any email address you choose to provide (legal basis: consent — GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), since submitting feedback is a voluntary action);
  • comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations (legal basis: legal obligation — GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or “share” (as defined under California law) your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or cross-context behavioral tracking.

4. How Information Is Shared

We share information only with the service providers (“subprocessors”) necessary to run the app, and only as needed:

  • Apple — processes your payments and provides purchase/subscription information (see Apple’s privacy policy).
  • Fly.io — hosts our application servers (United States).
  • Neon — hosts our database (United States).

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

International data transfers. Our servers and database are located in the United States. If you use the app from outside the United States, including from the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have different data-protection laws than your country.

5. Data Retention

We retain your anonymous account identifier and purchase records for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide the service and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations. Technical logs are retained only for a short period. Feedback you submit, including any email address you provide with it, is retained indefinitely for reference. You can request deletion as described below.

6. Your Rights (EEA/UK — GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request rectification of inaccurate data;
  • request erasure (“right to be forgotten”);
  • request restriction of processing;
  • data portability;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@hurutungo.com. Because accounts are anonymous, we may need information from you (such as your account token or the relevant purchase) to locate your data.

7. Your Rights (California — CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, delete, and correct the personal information we hold, and to be free from discrimination for exercising your rights.

In the past 12 months we have collected the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers — your anonymous account token, IP address, and, if you submit in-app feedback and choose to include one, your email address.
  • Commercial information — records of your purchases and subscription status.
  • User-generated content — the text of any feedback, bug report, or suggestion you submit in the app.

We collect this to provide and secure the app (see Section 2). We do not sell or share your personal information, and have not done so in the past 12 months. To exercise your rights, contact us at hello@hurutungo.com.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including storing access tokens in hashed form and encrypting data in transit. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be reflected here.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at hello@hurutungo.com.
