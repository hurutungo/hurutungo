Purrlog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Purrlog is a health tracker for cats, made by David Martinez (“we”). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app. The short version: we don’t collect any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. Purrlog has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software development kits. We never receive, see, sell or share your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter stays on your device. That includes your cats’ profiles, weigh-ins, litter box entries, medications, dose history, vet visits, vaccines, photos and health flags.

  • iCloud sync (Purrlog Pro only). If you buy Purrlog Pro and are signed in to iCloud, your data syncs between your devices through Apple’s iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. Only you can access it. We can’t read it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Photos. Purrlog only sees the photos you pick with the system photo picker. It never gets access to your photo library. The photos you pick are resized and stored inside the app.
  • Reminders. Medication and vaccine reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Purrlog asks permission before sending any.
  • Backups and vet summaries. Backup files and PDF vet summaries are created only when you ask for them. They are saved or shared wherever you choose, such as Files, Mail or AirDrop.

Purchases

The Purrlog Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro has been bought on your Apple ID.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. These contain technical information about the crash, not your cats’ data. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • To delete a cat and everything recorded for it, open the cat’s profile and choose Delete cat.
  • To delete everything on a device, delete the app.
  • To delete synced data, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, or delete the app on every device.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Purrlog is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date at the top.
