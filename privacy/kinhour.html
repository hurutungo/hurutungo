Kinhour Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Kinhour (“the app”) is made by hurutungo LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Kinhour. The short version: we don’t collect any of your data. The app has no account to sign up for, and we don’t run servers, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information you create in the app

The timers and events you create are stored in two places:

  • On your device, so the app works offline.
  • In your own iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit private database, so they sync between your devices.

This can include timer names, event names (for example, a medication name you type in), durations, schedules, and when doses were marked as taken. This data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot access it.

Sharing with other people

When you share a timer or event, Kinhour uses Apple’s iCloud sharing. The people you invite can see that timer or event and its history. Depending on the permission you choose, they can also pause, resume, stop or update it. Participants can see details provided by iCloud, for example who created or restarted a timer.

You control sharing. You can stop sharing, or remove participants, at any time. If you leave a shared item, your copy is removed from your devices; the item stays with everyone else.

Notifications and Live Activities

Timer and dose reminders are scheduled on your device. Kinhour also uses Apple’s push notification service so changes from people you share with can reach your device; these notifications carry no personal content. Lock Screen Live Activities are created and updated on your device.

Purchases

Kinhour Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payments, and we never receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Diagnostics

  • Diagnostics summary. Settings has a “Copy diagnostics” button that copies a short technical summary to your clipboard: app version, iOS version, device model, iCloud and notification status, subscription status, and how many timers and events you have. It contains no timer names or other content. It’s only shared if you paste it into a message to us yourself.
  • Crash reports. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may send us anonymized crash reports. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Data stored on your device

Kinhour stores a small identifier for your iCloud account on your device. If you sign in to a different iCloud account, the app uses it to avoid mixing data between accounts. It never leaves your device.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual timers and events in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • To remove Kinhour’s data from iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or “Apps Using iCloud”) › Kinhour.

Children

Kinhour is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
